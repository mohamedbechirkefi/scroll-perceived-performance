# results/

Machine-readable experiment outputs. Raw per-trial records go to `results/raw/<EXP-ID>/` and are not committed (see `.gitignore`); they are regenerated by the runner. Small summary tables that reports cite are committed with a manifest and SHA-256 checksums. Empty at M0: no experiment has run.
