# EXP-001: Smoke run and instrumentation validation

- Status: planned (protocol draft, awaiting owner freeze). Exploratory by design: its results are never used as confirmatory evidence.
- Version: draft v0.1, 2026-11-24. Protocol hash: not yet recorded (computed at freeze).
- Parent protocol: `research/protocol.md` draft v0.1.

## 1. Question

Does the harness measure what the protocol says it measures, and how noisy is it? Specifically: (a) is `TTUC_BLANK - d` (TTUC minus the injected delay) small, positive and stable; (b) what is the standard deviation of paired per-seed differences `sigma_d` used for the sample size; (c) does each arm behave as specified (placeholder appears, prefetch used, rollback correct)?

## 2. Mechanism and rationale

The mock transport delays each response by a known `d`. In `BLANK`, content cannot appear before `t_in + d`; the excess `TTUC - d` is rendering and scheduling overhead. A differential test against this known lower bound validates the timing chain before any comparison (W3C-ET and MDN-LCP in `research/literature_review.md` explain why standard metrics cannot be used directly for post-interaction content).

## 3. Competing explanations to rule out

Timer clamping, frame quantisation, garbage collection pauses, harness overhead in Playwright, headless rendering artefacts.

## 4. Hypotheses (instrumentation checks, not research hypotheses)

- C1: in `BLANK`, `TTUC >= d` for 100% of trials. Contradicted by any trial with `TTUC < d`.
- C2: median `TTUC - d` in `BLANK` is under 50 ms at both latency levels (planning threshold, to be justified or revised).
- C3: rollback correctness is 1.0 in `OPTIMISTIC` with `p = 0.5`.
- C4: in `PREFETCH` with `h = 150 ms`, the prefetched response is used in 100% of clicked trials.

## 5. Design

- Experimental unit: one session (fresh context) of 5 interactions.
- Variables: arm (independent), L in {100, 1000} ms, p in {0, 0.5} (write path only), seed in {1001..1005}.
- Dependent: TTUC, TTFF, Event Timing duration, layout shift sum, wrong-state exposure, harness wall-clock.
- Baseline: `BLANK`.
- Sample: read 3 arms x 2 L x 5 seeds x 5 = 150 interactions; write 2 arms x 2 L x 2 p x 5 seeds x 5 = 200 interactions.
- Compute budget: at most 30 minutes wall-clock on the M0 machine, CPU throttle 1x, headless.

## 6. Analysis

Descriptive only: per-cell medians, interquartile ranges, minimum of `TTUC - d`, `sigma_d` estimate per contrast with a bootstrap interval. No hypothesis tests on research contrasts.

## 7. Exclusion and stopping

As in the parent protocol, section 7. Any C1 or C3 failure stops the campaign and is recorded as a defect.

## 8. Anticipated failure modes

Double `requestAnimationFrame` may fire late in a background tab; headless Chromium may throttle timers; Playwright click dispatch may add variable delay between `t_in` and handler.
